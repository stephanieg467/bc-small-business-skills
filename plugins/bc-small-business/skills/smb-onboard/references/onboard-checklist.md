# Onboarding checklist

## Interview questions (one at a time)

1. **What's the legal name, and is it a corporation (Ltd./Inc.), a sole
   proprietorship or a partnership?** (If unsure: "Does the name end in Ltd. or Inc.?")
2. **When does the business's financial year end?**
3. **Is it registered for GST? How often do you file: annually, quarterly or monthly?**
   Quick Method or regular? (Unsure → "ask accountant"; it's on CRA correspondence.)
4. **Is it registered for BC PST? How often do you file on eTaxBC?**
5. **Employees: how many, what roles, how often are they paid, and where is payroll
   done** (Sage 50 payroll, a service, by hand)? WorkSafeBC account?
6. **Which bank account(s) does the business use?** (Bank name and last four digits only.)
7. **Where do sales come from?** Cannabis Cottage: Cova. How do Cova sales get into
   Sage 50 (daily or monthly summary entry, someone else, not yet)? Fun Water Pools:
   invoices from Sage 50? Card machine? E-transfers?
8. **Main suppliers?**
9. **Who's the accountant, and what do they do** (year-end, tax returns, GST, payroll)?
10. **Your top three headaches** with the books or the business right now?
11. **How do you like results:** Claude docs and sheets (default), Excel, PDF or Word?
