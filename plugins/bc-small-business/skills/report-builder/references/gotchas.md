# Report gotchas

- **Partial month vs full month** looks like a crash. Label or compare like for like.
- **Tax-inclusive sales.** Cova totals may include GST/PST. Revenue is pre-tax.
- **Same sale counted twice** across Cova and Sage 50. One source per metric.
- **Category names change** in Cova (a renamed category splits its history). Map
  old to new names and say so.
- **Summary vs detail.** Aged summaries net out credits. Use detail.
- **Percent of what.** State the denominator under every percentage.
- **Seasonal business, monthly comparison** (Fun Water Pools). Default to same
  period last year.
- **Unclosed period.** Numbers before month-end close are provisional. Say so.
- **Formatted Excel exports** include subtotal rows. Drop them before summing.
