# Payroll anomaly checks (BC)

Flags are raised, never silently corrected. Word each as:
**[Person], [date]: [what was found]. [What it means if left]. [Question].**
Example: "Jordan, 14 Nov: clocked in 10:02, no clock-out. Hours unknown. What time did the shift end?"

## Punches
- Missing in or out
- Overlapping shifts / duplicate entries
- Shift over 12 hours (double time applies; also check it's real)
- Shift crossing midnight (hours belong to the start day unless her policy differs)
- Shift under 2 hours (minimum daily pay: 2 hours; 4 if scheduled over 8)
- No meal break on a shift over 5 hours (BC requires a 30-minute meal break; it's
  paid if the employee must stay available)

## Hours
- Daily overtime missed (any day over 8 hours paid at straight time)
- Weekly overtime: over 40, counting only the first 8 hours each day
- Hours far above schedule or normal pattern (give both numbers)
- Zero hours for a regular employee
- Fewer than 32 consecutive hours off in a week (BC weekly rest; extra pay applies if worked)

## Pay setup
- Rate below current BC minimum wage (CAD 18.25 from 1 June 2026; check for updates)
- Rate changed since last run
- New person with no start date or rate on file
- Person paid last period, missing now (left? on leave? forgotten?)
- Salaried employee with overtime hours (salary doesn't remove overtime rights
  for most BC employees; ask)

## Stats, vacation, sick
- Stat in the period: who qualifies (employed 30 days and worked/earned wages on
  15 of the 30 days before), average day's pay calculated, premium for anyone who worked
- Vacation pay method applied consistently (4% / 6% after 5 years)
- Sick day paid for someone under 90 days' employment (not required) or not paid
  for someone over 90 days (required, up to 5 a year)

## Leaving employees
- Final pay timing: 48 hours (employer ended it) / 6 days (employee quit)
- Outstanding vacation pay paid out
- ROE needed

## Cross-checks
- Total hours vs scheduled hours
- Gross vs last period (explain changes over 10%)
- Cannabis Cottage: staff hours vs store opening hours (hours outside opening
  times need a reason, like a stock count)

## Overtime edge cases
| Situation | Handling |
|---|---|
| Rate change mid-week | Ask which rate applies to which hours; state it |
| Two rates (e.g. install vs shop) | Overtime at the rate of the hours worked in overtime, unless her policy differs. State it. |
| Overtime bank requested in writing | Bank instead of pay, at the overtime rate. Ask for the agreement. |
| Averaging agreement | Different daily rules. Ask for the agreement and its dates. |
| Vacation / stat days in an OT week | Not counted as hours worked for overtime. State it. |
