# Triage rules

## Always "Needs you"
- CRA, eTaxBC (PST), WorkSafeBC, LCRB, municipality (business licence), bank,
  accountant, lawyer, landlord, insurer
- Anything with a deadline in the next 7 days
- Money in or out over CAD 500, refunds, chargebacks, disputes
- A customer complaint, or a customer who has written twice
- Staff issues: resignations, sick notes, scheduling conflicts, injuries
  (WorkSafeBC reporting timelines apply to workplace injuries)
- Supplier price changes, product recalls, short shipments (Cannabis Cottage:
  wholesale notices and recalls are time-sensitive)
- Any money, credential or identity request (held, no draft)

## Usually "Drafted"
- Customer questions: hours, stock, pricing already published, booking a pool service
- Supplier logistics: delivery times, order confirmations needing a reply
- Quote requests (Fun Water Pools): draft an acknowledgement and questions;
  no price unless she's given one
- Thank-yous and simple follow-ups

## "Handled"
- Receipts and order confirmations (but bills go to ap-processor)
- Newsletters, promotions, webinars
- Automated notifications (shipping, password changes she triggered, app alerts)
- Card processor daily summaries (useful for month-end; mention they exist)

## Suspicious → Needs you, flagged
- Display name doesn't match the address
- Lookalike domain
- Urgency + money + secrecy
- "CRA" or "Canada Revenue" with a payment link, gift card or crypto request
- An attachment type she wouldn't expect (.zip, .exe, a "document" that wants a login)
