# Reply patterns

## Customer question (answerable)
Answer first, one or two sentences, sign off. "Hi Pat, yes, we're open until 11
on Friday. See you then! [Name]"

## Customer question (needs her input)
Acknowledge and say when she'll get back: "Thanks for asking. Let me check and get
back to you by tomorrow." Don't invent the answer.

## Quote request (Fun Water Pools)
Thank them, ask what's needed to quote (pool type, size, address/area, photos,
timing), and say when to expect the quote. No price.

## Complaint
Acknowledge, apologise for the experience (not admitting fault on facts unknown),
say what happens next, invite a call. Flag it in Needs you as well.

## Supplier logistics
Confirm, ask the one missing detail, short.

## Scheduling
Offer two or three times she has given you; never book.

## Cannabis Cottage limits
No health or effect claims, no "best", no lifestyle imagery language, no
promotions aimed at young people, nothing implying a product is safe for a
condition. Facts only: availability, price as listed, hours, location,
"ID required, 19+".

## Sign-off
Her first name and the business name. Phone number only if she includes it in her
own emails.
