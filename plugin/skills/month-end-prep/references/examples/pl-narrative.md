# Example month summaries (illustrative figures)

## Cannabis Cottage, October 2026

**October came in at CAD 9,850 net income, up 7% on September.**

Net sales were CAD 118,400 (after CAD 6,100 in discounts), up 4%. Dried flower was
still about half of sales, and pre-rolls grew fastest (+12%) after the new
supplier lineup arrived mid-month.

Gross margin slipped from 29.8% to 28.9%. Two things did it: the Thanksgiving
weekend promotion and a wholesale price increase on two top-selling SKUs that
hasn't been passed through to shelf prices yet.

Wages rose 9% because of the stat holiday (Thanksgiving) premium pay and one extra
shift during the long weekend. Card processing fees rose with sales, as expected.

Net income CAD 9,850 vs. CAD 9,200 in September.

**Watch next month:** whether the two price-increased SKUs need a shelf-price change;
the inventory adjustment for the damaged-product write-off (CAD 640) still needs the
journal entry; Remembrance Day stat pay will land in the November payroll.

## Fun Water Pools, October 2026

**October came in at CAD 3,200 net income, down from CAD 14,600 in September, but up on October last year (CAD 1,100).**

Sales were CAD 41,000, mostly pool closings and two equipment replacements. That's
the normal end-of-season drop, and it's ahead of last October because of the
heat-pump installs.

Gross margin held at 38%. Materials cost tracked sales.

Vehicle and fuel costs fell 22% as service routes wound down. Insurance was flat.

**Watch next month:** two invoices over 30 days (CAD 4,800 total) are worth a
reminder; the off-season means fixed costs will exceed sales until spring, so
check the cash forecast before ordering spring stock.
