# Invoice Chase

Who owes money, how they usually pay, and a reminder for each, drafted in her
voice. **Nothing sends without her yes.**

Read first: `references/common/ground-rules.md`, `references/common/business-profiles.md`,
`references/common/sage50-canada.md`, `references/common/zoho-mail.md`.

Mostly for **Fun Water Pools** (invoiced jobs). Cannabis Cottage is cash/card retail
and usually has no AR. If she runs this for Cannabis Cottage and there's nothing
over due, say so and stop.

## Step 1: Overdue invoices

Ask for Sage 50 **Customer Aged Detail** (or **Aged Overdue Sales Invoices Detail**) as
of today. Use **Detail** so credits and partial payments show. List every invoice
more than 1 day past its due date, grouped by customer. A customer with a credit
on account: show it, and net it only if she says so.

## Step 2: Check for recent payments

Before drafting, check that the customer hasn't just paid:
- Ask for the **bank statement or online banking deposit list for the last 14 days**
  (e-transfers, cheques, card payments), and the Sage 50 **Receipt Transaction
  Details** for the same days.
- Zoho Mail (if connected): search for remittance or e-transfer notifications from
  the customer in the last 14 days.
- Any likely payment → **"possibly paid, verify"**, excluded from drafting.
  E-transfers often arrive under a person's name, not the company's, so match on
  amount as well as name.

## Step 3: Score each customer

From the last 12 months of Receipt Transaction Details (or her knowledge):
`good payer` / `sometimes late` / `often late`. Rules in `references/tone-matching.md`.
Fewer than 3 invoices: `sometimes late`. Strata councils, property managers and
commercial customers often pay on a monthly cycle; she may know that, so ask.

## Step 4: Draft reminders

One email per customer, all their overdue invoices together. Tone from the score.
Include invoice number(s), amount(s) in CAD, original due date, days overdue,
and how to pay (**ask her once what to include: e-transfer address, cheque payable
to, card by phone**, and add it to the profile). One call to action. Write in her
voice: short, friendly, local-business plain. Examples in `references/examples/`.

## Step 5: Show and wait

Summary table first:

| Customer | Amount due | Days late | Tone | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Then each draft in full. Wait for "send these" or individual approvals.

## Step 6: After approval

- **Zoho Mail connected**: create drafts in the right business's mailbox (or send
  only if she explicitly says to send and the connector supports it).
- **Not connected**: give copy-ready text for each.
- Report what was drafted/sent and what was excluded (possibly paid, credits, disputes).

## Approval rules

- No draft or send without her explicit yes. One yes covers one named batch.
- Never chase a customer who isn't on the aged report.
- Never chase anyone flagged "possibly paid".
- A customer disputing an invoice gets a reply drafted about the dispute, not a reminder.

## After

One next step: "cash forecast" to see what these collections do to the next 30
days; "plan payroll" if collections are meant to cover a payroll gap.
