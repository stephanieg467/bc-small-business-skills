# Tone matching

## Score (last 12 months, at least 3 invoices)

| Score | Rule |
|---|---|
| good payer | On time or early on 75% or more of invoices |
| sometimes late | Late on 25 to 50%, or fewer than 3 invoices |
| often late | Late on more than 50% |

"On time" = paid on or before the due date.

## Tone

| Score | Tone | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| good payer | Gentle | Friendly, assumes it slipped through |
| sometimes late | Neutral | Polite, factual |
| often late | Firm | Direct, gives a pay-by date, no warmth, no threats |

## Subject lines
- Gentle: `Quick reminder: invoice #[N], CAD [amount]`
- Neutral: `Following up: invoice #[N], CAD [amount] past due`
- Firm: `Past due: invoice #[N], CAD [amount] ([X] days)`

## Every reminder has
Invoice number(s), amount(s), original due date, days overdue, how to pay. One call
to action. Gentle adds one line of grace ("I know it's a busy time of year"). Firm
adds one pay-by date.

## Several invoices
One email: list each invoice, then the total. Use the customer's score, not the
oldest invoice's.

## Never
Late fees, interest or collections threats unless she asks and her invoices state
those terms.
