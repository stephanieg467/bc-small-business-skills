# Cash forecast gotchas

- **Sales tax looks like income.** Cova deposits include GST and PST. If the
  forecast treats them as spendable, the remittance month comes as a shock. Always
  schedule the remittance as an outflow.
- **Card deposits are net of fees and lag.** Model processor timing, not sale date.
- **Weekend batching.** A Monday deposit can carry three days of card sales.
- **Payroll is two outflows.** Net pay on payday, plus source deductions (employee
  and employer share) to the CRA by the 15th of the next month.
- **AR summary hides credits.** Use Customer/Vendor Aged **Detail**.
- **Seasonality (Fun Water Pools).** A forecast built on summer months will be
  wildly optimistic in November. Use the same months last year.
- **Event spikes (Cannabis Cottage).** 4/20, long weekends and December don't
  repeat every month. Remove them from the base pattern and add them back on
  their dates.
- **Inventory buying cycles.** Wholesale orders are lumpy. Use her actual
  ordering pattern, not an average spread evenly.
- **Stale balance.** A balance "as of" two weeks ago needs those two weeks'
  activity added. Ask, or say the balance is stale.
- **A thin range isn't certainty.** Few data points can look precise. Say how many
  weeks or payments the range comes from.
